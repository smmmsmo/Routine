## Weekly Workout Routine

### Daily, non-negotiable (~10 min total)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Wake up | 5 min back-care block *(bird dog 8/side, side plank 20s/side, glute bridge ×15, hip flexor stretch 30s/side, cat-cow ×10)* |
| After breakfast | Kegels — 10 reps (5s hold, 5s release) |
| Every 45 min sitting | Stand + walk 2 min |
| Afternoon | Kegels — 10 reps |
| Before bed | Kegels — 10 reps + 7–8 hrs sleep |

Everything below sits **on top** of this.

### Saturday — Strength A
*Best time: after Fajr/early morning, or 5–6 PM*

1. Bodyweight squats — 3×15
2. Incline push-ups (hands on table/bed) — 3×10
3. Table rows (lie under sturdy table, pull chest up to edge) — 3×8
4. Glute bridge — 3×15
5. Side plank — 3×20s each side

*Rest 60–90s between sets. ~30 min total.*

### Sunday + Tuesday — Cardio
*Best time: morning before breakfast, or after Maghrib*

- Brisk walk 40 min — fast enough that talking is slightly hard
- Recovery day for the muscles

### Monday — Strength B
*Same time slot as Saturday*

1. Reverse lunges — 3×10 each leg
2. Incline push-ups — 3×12
3. Wall sit — 3×30s
4. Bird dog — 3×8 each side
5. Dead bug — 3×10 each side

### Wednesday — Strength C
*Same time slot*

1. Squats — 3×20
2. Push-ups (drop to knees when form breaks) — 3×8
3. Stair step-ups — 3×12 each leg
4. Superman hold — 3×15s
5. Plank — 3×30s

### Thursday — Long Cardio

- Brisk walk 50–60 min
- Weigh yourself + measure waist at navel. Log both.

### Friday — Rest

- No training. Full-body stretch, 10 min, whenever suits you.

### Structure & Principles

- **3 strength days** (Sat / Mon / Wed) — never back to back
- **3 cardio days** (Sun / Tue / Thu) — walking on the days between
- **1 full rest day** (Fri)
- **Daily** — back block + Kegels + sit-breaks

Pick **one** time slot — morning or evening — and stick to it for all six days. Consistency beats optimal timing by a wide margin, and a fixed slot removes the daily decision of whether to train.

**Progression:** when a set stops feeling hard, add 2–3 reps before making the movement harder. Once incline push-ups hit 3×15 easily, lower your hands to a chair, then the floor.

## Nutrition

### Rules of thumb

- **Water:** 3 litres a day. Most people underdrink and mistake it for hunger.
- **Eggs:** boiled only, never fried (*dim shiddho, bhaja na*). Boil 8–10 at a time, keep in the fridge.
- **Fish:** jhol/curry, not deep-fried (*mach bhaja na*). Light sear then gravy.
- **Vegetables:** cook covered, in their own water, minimal oil (*sobji dhaka diye, tel diye na*).
- **Rice:** 1 cup raw ≈ 3 cups cooked. A "2–2.5 cups/day" target is *cooked* rice — that's ~¾ cup raw rice/day total.

### Typical day (quick reference)

| Meal | What |
|---|---|
| Morning | 2–3 eggs + 1 roti or ¾ cup rice + vegetable |
| Midday | 1 cup rice + fish or chicken + dal + shak |
| Night | ¾ cup rice + dal + vegetable + egg or fish |
