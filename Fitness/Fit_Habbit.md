For comparison, an average **0.75 kg/week** would put the goal around **June 2027**; **0.4 kg/week** around **December 2027**. Those are rate-based scenarios, not predictions of what this menu will produce. Plateaus or breaks can move the date later. A November checkpoint on the central scenario is roughly **96 kg at month-end**, with early water-weight changes making the scale less predictable.

[CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/) supports gradual rather than rapid loss. As weight falls, energy needs can fall too, so a fixed menu does not guarantee the same weekly loss indefinitely ([NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/adult-overweight-obesity/eating-physical-activity)).

## Check progress

Review consistent morning-weight averages and waist trend after two weeks, then reassess monthly. Adjust the forecast from actual progress; do not cut food aggressively to meet a calendar date. A single high or low weigh-in is not a reason to change the plan. Persistent excessive hunger, dizziness or poor recovery calls for reviewing intake and health rather than cutting more food.
