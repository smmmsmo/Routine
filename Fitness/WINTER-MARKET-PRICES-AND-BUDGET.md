## 7. Food and Health Rules Linked to the Budget

- **Whole fruits + non-starchy vegetables:** Aim at least **400 g/day combined**; a practical allocation is ~150–250 g whole fruit + ~250–350 g vegetables. Potatoes do **not** count toward this non-starchy target.
- **Protein:** A practical starting goal for a resistance-training adult may be around **1.4–1.6 g/kg/day** (individualized by body weight and health). Premium meat is not necessary.
- **Salt & sugar:** WHO generally advises adults under **5 g salt/day** and free sugars under **10% of energy**, ideally lower. Read the existing [drinks/sugar guide](SUGAR-TEA-COFFEE-DRINKS.md).
- **Budget:** Fat loss is not achieved simply by buying certain vegetables. Overall calories, adequate protein, training and sleep matter.
