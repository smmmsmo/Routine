# Night Exercise Plan — Six Organized Days

**Goal:** Fat loss, stronger muscles, better posture, stamina, cardiovascular and sexual health.  
**Schedule:** 4 challenging workouts + 2 normal walk-and-stairs sessions + **Friday recovery**.  
**Time:** **60 minutes per active night** (6 days = 360 scheduled minutes/week; not all minutes are moderate-intensity cardio). **Equipment:** Mat and optional securely closed backpack with books.

**How to read the numbers:** Each rep should take about **2–3 seconds** (controlled down, steady up). The rep ranges fit one **35–50 second work interval**: use the **lower end in weeks 1–2**, the **upper end from week 5**, and a steady rhythm in between. If you reach the top of the range early, **slow the reps down or add load** — don't rush. If you can't reach the bottom of the range with clean form, use the easier variation. Holds are listed in seconds.

## Saturday — Legs & Glutes (60 min)

**8 min preparation:** 2 min brisk march + 1 min hip hinges (10–12 reps) + 1 min leg swings (10 per side) + 1 min easy squats (10–12 reps) + 1 min glute bridges (12–15 reps) + 1 min calf raises (15–20 reps) + 1 min easy ankle circles (10 per side).

**44 min main circuit:** Do the six movements below **in order, five rounds**. Each movement gets a **75-second work/rest slot**; use the ratios below. Rest 90 seconds after rounds 1–4. **Six moves × five rounds × 75 seconds = 37.5 min; four 90-second between-round breaks = 6 min → 43.5 min.**

| # | Exercise | Target per work interval | Easy → Hard |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squats | **12–16 reps** | Bodyweight → backpack squats (10–14 reps) |
| 2 | Reverse lunges | **8–12 reps per side** | Supported → alternating → loaded (6–10 per side) |
| 3 | Glute bridges | **15–20 reps** | Two legs → single-leg (8–10 per side) |
| 4 | Hip hinge / deadlift | **12–15 reps** | Bodyweight good morning → backpack Romanian deadlift (10–12 reps) |
| 5 | Calf raises | **20–25 reps** | Both feet → single-leg (12–15 per side) |
| 6 | Wall sit | **Hold 30–50 s** | Short hold → 45–55 s or 15–20 controlled squat pulses |

**8 min finish:** 2 min slow march + 6 min gentle stretching (30–40 s per side for quads, hamstrings, glutes, hip flexors, calves and lower back).

## Sunday — 7 Floors Down → Normal Walk → 7 Floors Up (~60 min)

| Order | Activity | Target |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Go downstairs from home** | **Descend 7 floors** slowly, holding the handrail — **3–4 min, ~105–140 steps** |
| 2 | **Normal outdoor walk** | **about 50–52 min** at a comfortable natural pace; no fast walking or running — **~4,000–5,700 steps** |
| 3 | **Return home by stairs** | **Climb 7 floors** at your comfortable pace — **3–4 min, ~105–140 steps** |

**iPhone tracking:** Keep the phone with you; use Apple Health to see **Steps** (and **Flights Climbed**, when recorded). Judge the walk mainly by **time**, not by chasing a fixed step count.

**Timing:** Descend **3–4 min**, walk **50–52 min**, ascend **3–4 min**; adjust the walking portion to total **~60 min** without rushing. **Estimated entire-session steps: ~4,200–6,000**, assuming 80–110 steps/min during the walk and roughly 15–20 steps per floor each way. Actual stair geometry, walking rhythm and iPhone detection vary. Stop if knee pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness occurs.

## Monday — Upper Body & Posture (60 min)

**8 min preparation:** 2 min brisk march + 1 min shoulder circles (10 each direction) + 1 min arm swings (10 per side) + 1 min easy wall push-ups (8–10 reps) + 1 min very light backpack rows (12–15 reps) + 1 min thoracic rotations (8–10 reps) + 1 min wrist and neck circles.

**44 min main circuit:** Six movements **× five rounds**. Each movement gets a **75-second work/rest slot**. **Six moves × five rounds × 75 seconds = 37.5 min; four 90-second between-round breaks = 6 min → 43.5 min.** Rest 90 seconds after rounds 1–4.

| # | Exercise | Target per work interval | Easy → Hard |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Push-ups | **8–12 reps** | Incline → floor (6–10 reps) → slow lowering (4–6 reps, ~3 s down) |
| 2 | Backpack bent-over rows | **12–15 reps** | Light backpack → gradually heavier (10–12 reps) |
| 3 | Pike push-ups | **6–10 reps** | Shallow → deeper (5–8 reps) |
| 4 | Plank shoulder taps | **20–24 taps total** (10–12 per side) | Elevated support → floor |
| 5 | Bear crawls | **10–14 steps each way** (or 30–40 s) | Small controlled steps → longer/faster controlled steps |
| 6 | Prone reverse snow angels | **12–15 reps** | Short range → full controlled range |

**8 min finish:** 2 min slow march + 6 min gentle stretching (30–40 s per side for chest, shoulders, triceps, upper back, lats and neck) + slow breathing.

## Tuesday — Full-Body HIIT (60 min)

**8 min preparation:** 2 min fast march + 1 min hip mobility (8 circles per side) + 1 min easy lunges (8 per side) + 1 min slow mountain climbers (10–12 per side) + 1 min arm circles (10 each direction) + 1 min easy ankle bounces (20–30) + 1 min easy squats (10–12 reps).

**44 min main circuit:** Six movements **× five rounds**. Each movement gets a **75-second work/rest slot**. Work hard in the effort interval; keep landings controlled. **Six moves × five rounds × 75 seconds = 37.5 min; four 90-second between-round breaks = 6 min → 43.5 min.** Rest 90 seconds after rounds 1–4.

| # | Exercise | Target per work interval | Easy → Hard |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mountain climbers | **30–40 total** (15–20 per side) | Slow → fast (40–50 total) |
| 2 | Squat thrusts / burpees | **8–12 reps** | Step-back thrust → no-push-up burpee (10–15 reps) |
| 3 | High knees | **50–70 steps total** | Fast march → running high knees (80–100 steps) |
| 4 | Skaters | **16–20 total** (8–10 per side) | Lateral steps → skater jumps (20–24 total) |
| 5 | Alternating lunges | **12–16 total** (6–8 per side) | Controlled reverse lunges → faster alternating (16–20 total) |
| 6 | Plank jacks | **20–30 reps** | Plank step-outs → plank jacks (30–40 reps) |

**8 min finish:** 2 min slow walk in place + 6 min relaxed leg stretching (30–40 s per side for quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves and hip flexors) and breathing.

**Joint-friendly option:** Use step-based variations instead of jumping if you need them; intensity can still be challenging.

## Wednesday — 7 Floors Down → Normal Walk → 7 Floors Up (~60 min)

| Order | Activity | Target |
|---:|---|---|

| 2 | **Normal outdoor walk** | **about 50–52 min** at a comfortable natural pace — **~4,000–5,700 steps**; track with iPhone |
| 3 | **Return home by stairs** | **Climb 7 floors** at your comfortable pace — **3–4 min, ~105–140 steps** |

Since you already climb seven floors in 3–4 minutes comfortably, there is no need for a reduced-floor beginner phase. Keep the pace controlled. **Estimated entire-session steps: ~4,200–6,000**; count with iPhone Health (Steps, optionally Flights Climbed). This estimate is a reference, not a minimum target.

**44 min main circuit:** Six movements **× five rounds**. Each movement gets a **75-second work/rest slot**. **Six moves × five rounds × 75 seconds = 37.5 min; four 90-second between-round breaks = 6 min → 43.5 min.** Rest 90 seconds after rounds 1–4.

| # | Exercise | Target per work interval | Easy → Hard |

## Friday — Full Recovery

**No hard workout and no mandatory walk.** Prioritize sleep and recovery before Saturday's next session. An easy 20–30 min walk is optional if you feel good.

## Eight-Week Easy-to-Hard Progression

For **Saturday, Monday, Tuesday and Thursday**: **8-min warm-up + ~44-min circuit + 8-min cool-down ≈ 60 minutes**. Six exercises × five rounds, 75 seconds per exercise including rest. Rest 90 seconds after rounds 1, 2, 3 and 4.

| Weeks | Work/exercise | Rest/exercise | Difficulty |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **1–2** | 35 sec | 40 sec | Easier variation; precise form; lower end of rep range |
| **3–4** | 40 sec | 35 sec | Controlled reps; regular variation; middle of rep range |
| **5–6** | 45 sec | 30 sec | Harder variation or small backpack load; upper end of rep range |
| **7–8** | 50 sec | 25 sec | Challenge gradually, keep form solid |

**Strength rule:** Make challenging reps controlled; don't race or train to failure. If you need longer rest, take it even if the session exceeds 60 minutes. Tuesday is the HIIT day; the other three workouts focus on quality strength repetitions.

**Sunday/Wednesday walking routine (from week 1 onward):**

| Order | Exercise | Target |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Stairs down | **7 floors**, typically **3–4 minutes**, ~105–140 steps, carefully |
| 2 | Outdoor walk | **about 50–52 minutes** at normal pace, ~4,000–5,700 steps; count with iPhone |
| 3 | Stairs up | **7 floors**, typically **3–4 minutes**, ~105–140 steps at your usual pace |

**Note:** You already handle the full 7-floor climb comfortably, so a floor-by-floor beginner program is unnecessary. Keep the route consistent; aim for **~4,200–6,000 steps/session** only as an estimate, not a required target. If the descent or ascent takes longer, reduce flat-walk time. Normal walking can be light activity rather than guideline-level moderate cardio; climbing stairs is your higher-effort segment. Take the stairs carefully.

**Progression rule:** Start at a level you can do with control, and repeat a stage if needed. Stair progression is a guide, not a deadline. For alternating exercises, share the work interval between sides; start the next round on the opposite side.

## Essential Rules

- **Fat loss:** Workouts and stairs support fat loss, but a sustainable calorie deficit is also necessary. Normal walking is useful even if less intense than brisk walking.
- **Muscles & posture:** Train with controlled technique and progressively harder resistance; training can improve posture, not change bone structure.
- **Sexual fitness & blood flow:** Aerobic and strength training can support vascular health and erectile function, but harder morning erections are not guaranteed.
- **Recovery:** Friday is off; sleep well. Do not force hard exercise through significant fatigue or pain.
- **Safety:** Stop for chest pain, dizziness, fainting or unusual severe breathlessness. Progress gradually, especially if new to vigorous training.
