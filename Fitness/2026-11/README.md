## The everyday pattern

**Saturday–Thursday:** about 10 minutes in the morning, then 30–45 minutes in the afternoon. Strength on Saturday/Monday/Wednesday; walking on Sunday/Tuesday/Thursday. **Friday: recovery**, including a break from pelvic-floor practice. Normal easy daily movement is fine.

Follow the same food portions on Friday. Start around **2,100–2,300 kcal/day**, usually around **90–120 g protein**, with **450 g trimmed vegetables and 120–150 g edible fruit**. These are estimates; recipes, brands, trimming and fish/chicken yield change the result. Tea/coffee additions count within the food plan.

## What success means this month

Aim to complete the routine consistently, walk more comfortably and see a gradual downward weight/waist trend. Roughly **1–3 kg less by month-end** is a possible planning range, not a promised result. The first week can fluctuate with water weight. Reaching 75 kg is a longer project.

Abdominal exercises train your muscles; they cannot reliably choose where fat comes off. Overall fat loss drives waist reduction. General fitness can support sexual health, but this routine cannot promise a particular erection quality, ejaculation time or testosterone increase. See the evidence and limits in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).
