## Portion review — 15 November, then 30 November

Use at least two reasonably consistent weeks before interpreting a plateau. A practical desired trend after early water shifts is roughly **0.25–0.75 kg/week**; actual results vary. Slower progress with a smaller waist and better strength can still be useful.

| What the records show | Action |
|---|---|
| Weight is gradually falling, hunger manageable, exercise stable | Keep portions and progression unchanged |
| Little/no weight decline across two comparable weekly averages and waist unchanged | First check serving size, oil, milk/sugar, snacks and missed activity. If following the plan most days, make ONE small change: reduce dry rice from 180 to 150 g/day, about 100 kcal less, OR add an easy-to-tolerate 10-minute brisk walk on three active days. Do not change both together |
| More than about 1 kg/week lost after the initial week, or persistent excessive hunger, fatigue, dizziness or worsening exercise performance | Add roughly 100–200 kcal/day, such as 30–50 g dry rice, and hold training progression. Persistent symptoms need clinical advice; do not keep cutting food |
| Weight is noisy but waist is trending down and fitness improving | Keep the plan another week before deciding |
| A session causes sharp pain or recovery is poor | Reduce the provoking exercise/sets or repeat the previous week; do not compensate by skipping meals |

After a change, allow another 10–14 days of consistent records. These are conservative review rules, not a diagnosis or a promise of a particular fat-loss rate. Do not keep subtracting food weekly. Retain eggs, dal/soy, vegetables and milk when making an initial small calorie adjustment.
