## Entry requirements (already covered in earlier phases)

Strong multivariable calculus, linear algebra, matrix methods, optimization, probability, mathematical statistics, proof writing, introductory real analysis, elementary algorithms and programming in Python/NumPy. For theoretical-CS blocks, induction, reductions, graphs, combinatorics and basic complexity vocabulary are required.

## Boundaries to make the timing honest

This is a **mathematics-focused selected advanced survey**, not 13 full graduate courses. We finish every item in [TOPICS.md](TOPICS.md) at its specified level; deep theory of diffusion SDEs, complexity lower bounds, causal identification, information-theoretic converses and formal cryptographic proofs receive introductions, with research depth deferred. Advanced coding is used to verify mathematics, not to add a separate GPU-engineering syllabus. Stanford CS336's full production-scale GPU assignments are **not required**.

## Finish criterion

Complete all weekly scope, derive major formulas/proofs independently, solve unseen representative problems and pass cumulative evaluation (target **≥80%**, with no critical proof or prerequisite gap). If the deadline arrives before mastery, extend the calendar rather than marking incomplete topics as finished.
