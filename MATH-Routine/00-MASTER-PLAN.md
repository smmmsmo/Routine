# Master plan — six phases, prerequisites and timing

This is the six-phase master map. [Phase 0](Phase-0/README.md), [Phase 2](Phase-2/README.md) and [Phase 3](Phase-3/README.md) have detailed schedules, topics and resources. Phase 1 remains to be built; actual study still follows 0 → 1 → 2 → 3.

## Why these phases

The goal is to progress from rusty school mathematics to **strong university-level foundations, selected graduate-level depth, deep AI/ML and CS mathematics and research readiness in a specialization**. No fixed schedule can promise original research discoveries or mastery of all modern mathematics.

### Chronological gates

```text
Phase 0: school algebra + logs + trig + functions + limit intuition + proof literacy
   |
   v   gate: reliable precalculus and elementary reasoning
Phase 1: calculus I/II + linear algebra I + discrete math/proofs + multivariable foundations
   |
   v   gate: compute, explain and prove core undergraduate results
Phase 2: analysis + algebra + probability + statistics + ODEs + optimization + algorithms
   |
   v   gate: independent rigorous upper-undergraduate work
Phase 3: deeper AI/ML math + information theory + theoretical CS/complexity
   |
   v   gate: derive models, prove algorithmic claims and read advanced texts
Phase 4: measure/functional analysis + stochastic processes + specialized graduate electives
   |
   v   gate: selected graduate mathematical theory and research paper fluency
Phase 5: focused research problems + replication + writing + mentorship/seminars
```

Order is **primarily sequential**. Interleave prerequisite-safe examples: small proofs in Phase 0; regressions after linear algebra/calculus; paper reading before Phase 5; specialist subjects may require Phase 4 material while Phase 3 is still active. Do not treat completion of Phase 2 as immediate comprehension of Phase 3—only readiness to *study* it.

## Topic inventory for later phases (not the detailed lesson plans)

### Phase 1 — undergraduate foundations (64 weeks)
- Differential and integral calculus: limits, continuity, differentiation, applications, integration, fundamental theorem, sequences/series.
- Linear algebra: systems, vector spaces, linear maps, bases, rank, determinants, eigenvalues, orthogonality, least squares, SVD.
- Discrete math and proofs: logic, sets, quantifiers, induction, combinatorics, elementary graph theory.
- Multivariable introduction: partial derivatives, gradients, chain rule and multiple integrals.
- Programming integration: numerical derivatives/integrals, linear solvers, basic algorithm correctness proofs.

### Phase 2 — advanced undergraduate mathematics (72 weeks)

**Detailed advance-built plan:** [72-week schedule](Phase-2/SCHEDULE.md), [full selected syllabus](Phase-2/TOPICS.md), [quality-first resources](Phase-2/RESOURCES.md).
- Real analysis, metric spaces, sequences/series of functions, rigorous limits/integration.
- Abstract algebra: groups, rings, fields; proof-based linear algebra.
- Probability, mathematical statistics, inference and statistical modeling.
- Differential equations, numerical analysis, convex optimization and algorithms.
- Selected breadth: complex analysis, topology, number theory, Fourier methods.

### Phase 3 — mathematics for AI/ML and theoretical CS (64 weeks)

**Detailed advance-built plan:** [64-week schedule](Phase-3/SCHEDULE.md), [full scoped syllabus](Phase-3/TOPICS.md), [high-quality paid/free resources](Phase-3/RESOURCES.md).
- Matrix calculus, gradient/Jacobian/Hessian, autodifferentiation; numerical optimization.
- Statistical learning: likelihood, ERM, generalization bounds, kernels, SVMs, PCA.
- Deep learning theory: backprop, attention, transformers, generative models, reinforcement learning.
- Information theory, graphical models, probabilistic inference and robustness.
- Algorithms, randomized algorithms, automata, computability, P/NP, reductions, complexity.

### Phase 4 — graduate mathematical gateways (64 weeks)
- Measure and Lebesgue integration, function spaces, functional analysis.
- Advanced probability, conditional expectation, martingales and stochastic processes.
- Advanced optimization, convex duality; chosen geometry/PDE/topology/harmonic analysis electives.
- Graduate-level reading, proof reconstruction, mathematical seminars.

### Phase 5 — research focus (32 weeks + ongoing)
- Choose one research domain (e.g., learning theory, optimization, algorithms, probability, analysis).
- Map literature; reproduce and critique important proofs/experiments; formulate scoped questions.
- Seek qualified mathematical feedback; document failed approaches and assumptions.
- Submit a polished research-style report, open-source reproducible artefacts or formal proofs.
- Original publishable contributions are **not guaranteed** by the timeline.

## Dependency examples

- Algebra + trig → limits → calculus → multivariable calculus → optimization → neural-net gradients.
- Linear systems → vector spaces → eigenspaces/SVD → PCA → matrix optimization.
- Sets + logic → proof methods/induction → combinatorics → algorithms → complexity.
- Calculus + counting → probability → mathematical statistics → ML generalization.
- Real analysis → measure theory → graduate probability → stochastic calculus.

## Phase gate standard

For each course: explain definitions, solve unseen problems, reconstruct core proofs/derivations, apply to coding where relevant and pass cumulative review. Advance at **≥80%** with no essential prerequisite gaps. Phase transitions are a review decision, not an automated clock.
