# Phase 0 — exact 16-week / 112-day schedule

**Budget:** 16 × 21 = **336 scheduled hours**. **Day 1–6:** 20 min recall + 55 min new theory + 75 min exercises + 30 min proof/coding/derivation. **Day 7:** 30 min recall + 90 min mixed test + 45 min corrections + 15 min next-week plan. Days may be moved around, but each week has six lessons and one review day.

**Exercise targets:** 35–55 independently attempted problems/week (calculus/proofs weeks may be fewer but deeper), of which at least 8 must be non-routine or mixed. Record sources and problem numbers, not just checkboxes. A problem only counts after an unaided solution or documented correction. Use the [practice bank](PRACTICE-BANK.md) and textbook exercises. All tasks are targets, not rigid deadlines.

**Every day:** log date, topic, actual minutes, exercises attempted/corrected, one error, and next review date. Check the [tracker](ROUTINE-AND-TRACKER.md). Review earlier algebra even during later trig/calculus weeks.

## Fallback and acceleration rules

- If a weekly exam <70% on prerequisite skills, repeat the weak week rather than advancing.
- If 70–79%, spend the next week's first 3–5 hours on errors and re-test.
- If >=80% with solid no-notes retention, continue; optional extension problems are allowed.
- A skipped day is logged as 0 minutes, not silently counted. Catch up or extend dates; do not invent hours.
- Weekly rest is allowed: temporarily reduce intensity if needed; six-year hours are a planning model.
- During weeks 15–16, allocate priority to weaknesses surfaced by the diagnostic and mock exam.
