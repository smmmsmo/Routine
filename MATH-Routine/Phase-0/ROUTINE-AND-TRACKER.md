# Phase 0 — Daily routine, revision system and tracker

**Scheduled workload:** 3 hours/day × 7 days/week = 21 hours/week = 336 hours across 16 weeks. Actual studied hours can differ; track honestly. This is a **mathematics-only plan** and is not silently compatible with the old all-subject 19–23-hour engineering routine.

## Default 180-minute learning day (Days 1–6)

| Segment | Minutes | What to do |
|---|---:|---|
| Closed-book retrieval | 20 | 2 old algebra/log/trig problems + 1 definition |
| New concepts and derivations | 55 | Read textbook / selectively watch lecture / work example |
| Independent exercises | 75 | Routine → mixed → non-routine; no solutions until a real attempt |
| Proof, coding, error correction | 30 | Explain a theorem, build a visualization or repair errors |
| **Total** | **180** | **3 hours** |

When stuck, try simpler cases and write your approach. Use one hint only after honest effort. Rewrite the solution unaided later.

## Day 7 — review and exam day (180 minutes)

| Segment | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Closed-book recall from Weeks 1 to present | 30 |
| Mixed timed assessment (no notes) | 90 |
| Mark, correct and retry errors | 45 |
| Plan next week's priorities and spaced reviews | 15 |

A low-intensity review is still study. If ill or under exam pressure, reduce volume and extend the calendar rather than pretending three hours happened.

## Weekly time and checking

- 6 learning days = 18 hours; 1 review day = 3 hours; **21 hours planned**.
- Every 4th week: reduce new theory, increase cumulative work.
- Target ~35–55 independent exercise attempts per week when appropriately sized; proof-heavy weeks may have fewer but deeper problems.
- On Sunday, record **planned 21h vs actual h** and top 3 issues; aim for sustainable progress, not unbroken perfection.
- **Do not increase math to 21h while maintaining the old 5–7h math allocation inside the 19–23h overall engineering routine.** Decide which plan owns the week.

## Spaced review schedule

For each important formula, identity or difficult problem:
- Same day: one closed-book reconstruction.
- +2 days: 1–2 problems with changed values.
- +7 days: mixed recall without looking at chapter labels.
- +21 days: transfer problem or derive identity again.
- Monthly: 60–90-minute cumulative exam.

Flashcards are suitable for conditions, definitions and key unit-circle values, but **not** a substitute for solving problems or writing proofs.
