# Phase 0 — Diagnostics, qualifying exam and retention check

**Instructions:** Use paper, a pen and (only if a question explicitly requires it) a basic calculator. **Closed book and no AI.** Write complete steps; an unexplained number may receive reduced credit. Do NOT open [the answer key](ANSWER-KEY.md) before finishing an attempt. After marking, log mistakes and re-test weak areas with fresh problems.

## Diagnostic A — before Week 1

**20 questions × 5 points = 100 points; 75 minutes.** This test is for placement, not passing. If you know it already, you may accelerate that topic; if you merely recognize it, work it again.

### Numbers and algebra (D1–D7)
1. Calculate `3/4 - 5/6` exactly.
2. Evaluate `2^(-3)`.
3. Solve `2(x-3) = 3x + 1`.
4. Simplify `(x^2-9)/(x-3)` and state the forbidden input.
5. Solve `x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0`.
6. Solve `|2x-1| = 5`.
7. Solve the inequality `-2x+3 < 7`.

### Functions (D8–D10)
8. Find the inverse of `f(x)=3x-2`.
9. State the real domain of `f(x)=sqrt(x-4)`.
10. Find the roots of `f(x)=x^2-4x-5`.

### Exponential/logarithmic (D11–D14)
11. Find `log_2(32)`.
12. Solve `log_3(x)=2`.
13. State the product law for `log_a(xy)` and its conditions.
14. Solve `2^x=8`.

### Trigonometry (D15–D18)
15. Evaluate `sin(pi/6)`.
16. Evaluate `cos(pi)`.
17. Evaluate `tan(pi/4)`.
18. Convert 150 degrees to radians.

### Calculus readiness (D19–D20)
19. Evaluate `lim_(x→2) (x^2-4)/(x-2)`.
20. Differentiate `x^3` with respect to x.

**Diagnostic interpretation:** Under 70% in any grouping = red prerequisite; 70–84% = yellow; 85%+ with confident explanation = provisional green, pending seven-day recall.

## Exit Exam B — Week 16

**25 questions × 4 points = 100 points; 120 minutes.** Each of the first four categories contains 5 questions / 20 points. **Pass requires ≥80/100 overall and ≥14/20 in *each* of A, F, L, T.** If marking 4 points per fully correct question, 16/20 is the first achievable passing category score. Partial credit is allowed only for valid written reasoning, and critical domain errors must be repaired.

### Category A — Algebra (E1–E5)
1. Solve `(2x-1)/3 - (x+2)/2 = 1`.
2. Solve `x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0`.
3. Simplify `(x^2-16)/(x^2-8x+16)`; state exclusions.
4. Solve `(x-3)(x+1)<0`.
5. Solve `sqrt(x+5)=x-1`; check for extraneous solutions.

### Category F — Functions (E6–E10)
6. Find the range of `f(x)=(x-2)^2+1`.
7. Find the inverse of `f(x)=(x-1)/4`.
8. For `g(x)=1/(x-2)`, state domain and vertical/horizontal asymptotes.
9. If `f(x)=x^2+1` and `g(x)=2x-3`, evaluate `f(g(2))`.
10. Is `h(x)=2x` for `x<1` and `h(x)=x+1` for `x>=1` continuous at `x=1`? Explain using one-sided values.

### Category L — Exponents and logarithms (E11–E15)
11. Solve `log_2(8x)=5`; state domain.
12. Solve `log_3(x)+log_3(x-2)=1`; reject invalid roots.
13. Solve `5^(2x)=125`.
14. Expand `log_b(u^3/v)` for `u,v>0` and `b>0,b≠1`.
15. Solve `ln(x-1)=0`; state domain.

### Category T — Trigonometry (E16–E20)
16. Evaluate `sin(5pi/6)` exactly.
17. Evaluate `cos(7pi/6)` exactly.
18. Evaluate `tan(3pi/4)` exactly.
19. Justify `sin^2(theta)+cos^2(theta)=1` using unit-circle geometry.
20. Solve `sin(x)=1/2` for `0<=x<2pi`.

### Category C — Calculus and reasoning (E21–E25)
21. Evaluate `lim_(x→3) (x^2-9)/(x-3)`.
22. Use the difference quotient to derive the derivative of `x^2`.
23. For `f(x)=3x^2 ln(x)`, `x>0`, find `f'(x)`.
24. Prove by induction that `1+3+...+(2n-1)=n^2` for positive integers n.
25. Use a **forward** difference to approximate the derivative of `f(x)=x^2` at x=2 with h=0.01. Give the value and compare with the exact derivative.

### Separate oral/written check (not included in 100 points)
- Explain why `log(x+y)=log(x)+log(y)` is generally false.
- Explain which restrictions can be lost when cancelling rational expressions.
- Reconstruct the chain rule's intuition, and one short proof or counterexample.
- Demonstrate and explain your Phase 0 Python mini-project.

**Exam completion:** date __ | raw score __/100 | A __/20 | F __/20 | L __/20 | T __/20 | C __/20 | project completed [ ] | proof accepted [ ]. If failing, repair the exact prerequisite and take a fresh alternative assessment.

## Seven-day retention check — after the exit exam

**10 questions × 10 points = 100; 40 minutes; new values.** Complete 7 days after the main gate; no answers or saved notes.

1. `5/6 - 1/4`.
2. Solve `4x-7=2x+9`.
3. Factor `x^2-x-12`.
4. Invert `f(x)=2x+5`.
5. Evaluate `log_4(64)`.
6. Evaluate `ln(e^(-2))`.
7. Evaluate `cos(2pi/3)`.
8. Evaluate `tan(5pi/4)`.
9. Evaluate `lim_(x→1) (x^2-1)/(x-1)`.
10. Differentiate `5x^3-2x`.

**Retention pass:** >=80/100, with no repeated critical algebra/log/trig failure. If not, repair and use new variants after another week.
