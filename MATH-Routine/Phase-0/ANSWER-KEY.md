## Exit Exam B

### A — Algebra
- **E1:** Multiply by 6: `2(2x-1)-3(x+2)=6`, hence `x=14`.
- **E2:** `(x-2)(x-5)=0`; roots 2,5.
- **E3:** `(x-4)(x+4)/(x-4)^2=(x+4)/(x-4)`, with `x≠4`.
- **E4:** Product negative between roots: `-1<x<3`.
- **E5:** Domain requires `x>=1`. Squaring gives `x^2-3x-4=0` → candidates 4 and -1; only `x=4` works.

### F — Functions
- **E6:** `[1, infinity)`, since square >=0.
- **E7:** `f^-1(x)=4x+1`.
- **E8:** Domain `R\{2}`, vertical x=2, horizontal y=0.
- **E9:** `g(2)=1`, so `f(g(2))=2`.
- **E10:** Yes; left limit =2, right limit =2, and h(1)=2.

### L — Logarithms
- **E11:** `x=4` (domain x>0).
- **E12:** `x>2`; `x(x-2)=3`, roots 3,-1; only `x=3`.
- **E13:** `2x=3` → `x=3/2`.
- **E14:** `3log_b(u)-log_b(v)`.
- **E15:** `x>1`; `x-1=1`, so `x=2`.

### T — Trigonometry
- **E16:** `1/2`.
- **E17:** `-sqrt(3)/2`.
- **E18:** `-1`.
- **E19:** A point on the unit circle at angle theta has coordinates (cos theta, sin theta); by x²+y²=1, cos²theta+sin²theta=1.
- **E20:** `x=pi/6` or `x=5pi/6` within the requested interval.

### C — Calculus/reasoning
- **E21:** Factor `(x-3)(x+3)`; limit is 6.
- **E22:** `[(x+h)^2-x^2]/h=2x+h` for nonzero h; as h→0 derivative =2x.
- **E23:** `f'(x)=6x ln(x)+3x`, by product rule.
- **E24:** Base n=1: 1=1². Assume first n odds sum n²; add next odd 2n+1 to get n²+2n+1=(n+1)². Therefore all positive integers by induction.
- **E25:** `[(2.01)^2-2^2]/0.01 = 4.01`. Exact derivative of x² at x=2 is 4; error +0.01.
