# Phase 2 — Advanced Undergraduate Mathematics

**Status:** Curriculum designed. **Study order:** Phase 0 → Phase 1 → Phase 2 (start Phase 2 when its prerequisites are met). This document is a syllabus, **not** a claim that Phase 2 has already begun.

## 1. Fixed timeframe

- **72 weeks** = **504 study days** at 7 days/week.
- **3 hours/day** = **21 hours/week** = **1,512 scheduled hours**.
- Approximate calendar length: **16.6 months** (~1 year 4.5 months) assuming no missed days.
- **Week 1 means the first week AFTER passing Phase 1**, not the present calendar week.
- All reading, problem-solving, proof writing, coding and review must fit inside these 1,512 hours. Do not add another course on top.

Complex analysis is an intensive introduction, not the whole of graduate complex analysis. This is how all scheduled topics fit honestly within the fixed time budget.

## 4. Weekly time formula (part of scheduled hours)

- Days 1–6: 3 hours/day focused on that week's listed topics and textbook exercises.
  - Approx. **6 hours/week** reading/lectures and notes.
  - Approx. **10 hours/week** independent exercises and proofs/derivations.
  - Approx. **2 hours/week** application, implementation or difficult problems.
- Day 7: **3 hours** cumulative revision and a short, closed-book test.
- **Total: 21 hours/week**, never 24. Each block's final week includes its assessment; final 3 weeks consolidate all subjects.

## 5. Prerequisites (must be ready before Week 1)

- Calculus I/II including series, multivariable differential/integral calculus.
- Linear algebra: vector spaces, linear maps, eigenvalues, inner products, least squares.
- Discrete mathematics: sets, relations, induction, combinatorics, graph basics.
- Proof techniques: direct, contrapositive, contradiction, induction and clear mathematical writing.
- Programming basics sufficient for numerical methods and algorithm implementations.

## 6. Completion standard

Completion means finishing **all specifically listed weekly topics** and independently solving representative exercises, including proofs where appropriate. In Week 72, complete a cumulative exam aiming for **≥80%**, and repair essential gaps before beginning Phase 3. Calendar completion alone is not mastery.

**Resource access:** university-provided paid textbooks/lectures are welcome; select based on mathematical quality and the level of the course, not price.
