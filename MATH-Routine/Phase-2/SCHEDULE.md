# Phase 2 — Exact 72-week timetable

**Read [TOPICS.md](TOPICS.md) for precise subtopics and [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md) for book/lecture links and the resource codes below.** This timetable assigns **all Phase 2 topics to weeks 1–72**: 21 hours per week, including exercise time and review, not 21 hours plus homework. Every week includes 6 × 3-hour learning sessions + 1 × 3-hour review/cumulative quiz. A block's final week includes a block test; last 3 weeks are reserved for consolidation.

**For each 21-hour week:** about 6 h primary reading/lectures, 10 h exercises and proof derivations, 2 h coding/deep problems, 3 h revision/assessment. The proportions may vary by subject but total always remains **21 hours**.

## Total and finish condition

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Assigned weeks | **72** |
| Study days (7/week) | **504** |
| Hours (21/week) | **1512** |
| Blocks | **12** |

**Finish within this timeframe:** every week has a defined topic set and output; the final 3 weeks are fully inside the 72-week budget. Choose representative book problems that cover the target ideas—**do not attempt every chapter or every optional reference**. A 72-week time allocation is an achievable *coverage plan* under the stated prerequisites, not proof that all learners will master it by the deadline. If a critical prerequisite has not been mastered, extend the phase rather than pretending the material is understood.
