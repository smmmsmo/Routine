**Curriculum planned ahead.** Actual learning sequence remains Phase 0 → Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3 → **Phase 4** → Phase 5. This document does **not** mean Phase 4 is underway.

## 1. Entire timeframe

- **64 weeks × 21 hours/week = 1,344 scheduled study hours.**
- **3 hours/day × 7 days/week = 448 planned study days.**
- Calendar equivalent **approximately 14.7 months** at a constant daily schedule, excluding pauses and repairs.
- Phase 4 Week 1 starts **only after passing Phase 3** and confirming the prerequisites.
- All textbook reading, exercises, proofs, coding/simulations and revision fit **inside** this budget.

## Prerequisites

- Proof-based analysis: sequences, continuity, compactness, metric spaces, uniform convergence.
- Abstract linear algebra, spectral basics, multivariable differentiation, ordinary differential equations.
- Calculus-based probability, mathematical statistics, conditional expectation basics.
- Numerical analysis, optimization foundations, advanced probability/ML mathematics from Phase 3.
- Selected topology/geometry groundwork; Chapter 0 style review is scheduled before differential geometry.

## Resource and depth policy

Choose the **best teaching texts for the goal**, irrespective of price; university subscriptions and good author-open textbooks are equally acceptable. One primary spine per block, optional difficult reference.

**Critical depth boundary:** 64 weeks cannot yield comprehensive mastery of measure theory, functional analysis, modern probability, stochastic calculus, geometry, PDE and harmonic analysis simultaneously. The goal is to **complete every explicitly listed graduate gateway topic at its assigned depth**, with coherent selected proofs and exercises, and then choose **one specialty for deeper Phase 5 research**. We do not require finishing all chapters of every graduate book.

## Weekly time allocation (21 h, inclusive)

- 6 h selected reading/lectures and reconstructing definitions.
- 10 h independent proof/problem solving and calculations.
- 2 h mathematical coding/simulations or a deep challenge.
- 3 h cumulative review/assessment.
- **21 h total** including all exercises; no “extra hidden homework hours”.

## Phase 4 finish condition

All 64 weeks' listed topics covered, original written proofs of core theorems reconstructed with assumptions, closed-book cross-topic assessment target **≥80%**, selected proofs/experiments checked, weak prerequisites repaired. Completing 64 calendar weeks without mastery does not unlock research specialization automatically.
