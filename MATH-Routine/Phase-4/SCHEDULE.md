# Phase 4 — Exact 64-week graduate mathematics schedule

**Start only after Phase 3.** Every required subtopic from [TOPICS.md](TOPICS.md) is assigned to a week. Resource codes reference [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md). One calendar week = **7 × 3 h = 21 hours**, including all reading, problem sets, proof work, coding and review.

**Weekly budget:** 6 h selected primary reading/lectures + 10 h independent proof/problem solving + 2 h coding/deep challenge + 3 h cumulative review/closed-book weekly assessment = **21 h total**. Every block's final week includes its own checkpoint. **No parallel full graduate books** are assumed; select only the needed sections for that week's scope.
