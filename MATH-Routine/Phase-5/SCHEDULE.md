# Phase 5 — Complete 32-week mathematical research schedule

**Phase 5 is a single-specialty apprenticeship, not a survey of all research disciplines.** Select **one** T1–T5 track from [TOPICS.md](TOPICS.md), then study the relevant sections/papers in [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md). The shared research methodology spans all 32 weeks.

**Time: 21 hours per week, total 672 hours.** Default weeks: approximately **6 hours** specialized texts/papers, **10 hours** proofs/derivations/controlled experiments, **2 hours** verification or presentation, and **3 hours** writing/review/feedback. In Weeks 27–32, move more of the 10 proof/derivation hours into writing and revision while preserving **21 total hours**. The weekly output is included in this allocation, not extra homework.

**Start:** after Phase 4 mastery and a selected research topic. Week numbers are relative to entry, not calendar dates. Mentor/reading-group engagement is recommended when available but not guaranteed.

**What counts as finishing:** a well-attributed, mathematically correct 12–20-page research-style report **when the material supports it**, a selected result independently reproduced, a scoped investigation, explicit failures/limitations, and a follow-up research plan. **Original publishable work is NOT guaranteed** and can require additional years, external critique and deeper specialization.

**If novelty fails:** pivot to an accurate exposition/reproduction/negative finding without inventing results. If a key proof is incomplete, label it incomplete. Never equate 32 weeks with automatic research mastery.
