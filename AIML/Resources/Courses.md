## Suggested Course Sequence

1. Mathematics lectures alongside Month 1–2 study.
2. One classical ML course during Months 3–4.
3. One deep learning course during Month 5.
4. CS231n during Month 6.
5. CS224N and Hugging Face during Months 7–8.
6. David Silver or CS234 during Month 10.
7. Full Stack Deep Learning during Month 11.

## Completion Rule

A lecture is not complete until you have:

- written concise notes;
- solved selected exercises;
- implemented at least one important idea;
- connected it to a project;
- reviewed it after one week.
