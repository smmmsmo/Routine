# Natural Language Processing

## Text Foundations

- Unicode, normalization, sentence splitting, tokenization, stemming, and lemmatization
- Word, character, and subword vocabularies; BPE, WordPiece, and unigram concepts
- Bag-of-words, n-grams, TF-IDF, feature hashing, and classical text classifiers
- Language-modeling objectives, perplexity, and data quality

## Representations and Sequence Models

- Distributional semantics and cosine similarity
- Word2Vec, GloVe, and FastText
- RNNs, vanishing gradients, LSTMs, and GRUs
- Sequence-to-sequence models, teacher forcing, beam search, and attention
- Contextual embeddings and transfer learning

## Transformers

- Self-attention, masking, multi-head attention, positional information
- Encoder-only, decoder-only, and encoder-decoder models
- BERT-style masked language modeling and GPT-style causal language modeling
- Fine-tuning, prompting, adapters, and sentence transformers

## Tasks and Evaluation

- Text classification and sentiment analysis
- Named-entity recognition and sequence labeling
- Semantic similarity, retrieval, and clustering
- Machine translation, summarization, and question answering
- BLEU/ROUGE concepts and their limitations; task-specific and human evaluation
- Toxicity, bias, multilingual performance, robustness, and privacy concerns

## Projects

- TF-IDF versus transformer text classifier
- Named-entity recognition system
- Semantic search engine
- Extractive or generative question answering
- Summarization application with factuality checks
