# Probability

## Topics

- Sample spaces, events, axioms, and counting
- Conditional probability, independence, total probability, and Bayes' theorem
- Discrete and continuous random variables
- PMF, PDF, CDF, expectation, variance, covariance, and correlation
- Joint, marginal, and conditional distributions
- Bernoulli, binomial, categorical, multinomial, Poisson, uniform, exponential, Gaussian, and multivariate Gaussian distributions
- Law of large numbers and central limit theorem
- Transformations of random variables
- Entropy, cross-entropy, KL divergence, and mutual information
- Graphical-model and Bayesian-network basics

## ML Connections

- Probabilistic predictions and uncertainty
- Naive Bayes and generative classifiers
- Gaussian models and mixture models
- Cross-entropy losses
- Bayesian inference
- Information theory in representation learning and language modeling

## Practice

- Simulate common distributions
- Verify the law of large numbers and central limit theorem
- Implement Bayes' theorem examples
- Compute entropy, cross-entropy, and KL divergence
- Visualize covariance and multivariate Gaussian samples

## Mastery Checklist

- [ ] I can distinguish independence from zero correlation.
- [ ] I can manipulate joint, marginal, and conditional probabilities.
- [ ] I understand expectation as a weighted average.
- [ ] I can explain common distributions and when they are appropriate.
- [ ] I can explain entropy and KL divergence without relying only on formulas.
- [ ] I can use simulation to validate probability calculations.
