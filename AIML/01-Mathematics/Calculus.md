# Calculus

## Topics

- Functions, limits, continuity, and local approximation
- Derivatives and their geometric meaning
- Product, quotient, and chain rules
- Partial derivatives and multivariable functions
- Gradients, directional derivatives, Jacobians, and Hessians
- Taylor series and second-order approximation
- Integrals, expectations, and accumulated quantities
- Constrained optimization and Lagrange multipliers
- Matrix-calculus notation used in ML

## ML Connections

- Gradients for training models
- Chain rule and backpropagation
- Curvature, conditioning, and optimization speed
- Jacobians in vector-valued models
- Hessians and second-order methods
- Regularization and constrained objectives
