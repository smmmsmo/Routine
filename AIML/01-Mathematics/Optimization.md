# Optimization

## Topics

- Objective functions, parameters, constraints, and feasible regions
- Convex sets, convex functions, local and global minima
- Gradient descent and stochastic gradient descent
- Mini-batches, momentum, Nesterov momentum, RMSProp, Adam, and AdamW
- Learning rates, schedules, warmup, and line-search intuition
- Conditioning, saddle points, plateaus, exploding gradients, and vanishing gradients
- L1/L2 regularization and constrained optimization
- Lagrange multipliers and KKT intuition
- Coordinate descent, Newton methods, and quasi-Newton methods
- Early stopping and implicit regularization
- Hyperparameter search: grid, random, Bayesian, and successive halving concepts

## ML Connections

- Training regression models and neural networks
- Regularization and sparse solutions
- Optimizer selection and training stability
- Resource-aware hyperparameter tuning

## Practice

- Implement batch and stochastic gradient descent
- Compare momentum, RMSProp, and Adam on the same objective
- Visualize optimization trajectories on contour plots
- Study the effect of feature scaling and conditioning
- Implement early stopping and learning-rate scheduling

## Mastery Checklist

- [ ] I can explain why scaling affects optimization.
- [ ] I understand convexity and why deep learning is generally non-convex.
- [ ] I can diagnose learning rates that are too high or too low.
- [ ] I can explain momentum and adaptive optimizers.
- [ ] I understand how regularization changes an objective.
- [ ] I can design a fair hyperparameter search.
